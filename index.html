FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Reach out to our support team.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — the Starter plan is free forever and includes up to 3 projects, basic task boards, and 1 GB of storage. No credit card required.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your account settings — changes apply immediately.

Does Nova work for large teams?

Yes. Our Enterprise plan includes unlimited members, advanced permissions, and dedicated support built for larger organizations.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and every plan includes role-based permissions to control who sees what.
